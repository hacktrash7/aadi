# Progress Tracker

A living checklist + reflection log. The kid checks the boxes. The parent
writes 1–2 lines per week about what they noticed. Together you'll have an
honest record of the journey.

> **Tip:** print this once a month and stick it on the fridge. Visible
> progress is motivating progress.

---

## How to use this file

- **Checkboxes** (`- [ ]` → `- [x]`): the kid ticks them after they
  genuinely meet the criterion (not "I watched it" — *"I can do it"*).
- **Weekly reflection** (bottom of file): parent writes 1–2 lines after
  the Friday session.
- **Stuck list**: any time the kid hits something they don't understand,
  write it in the Stuck List at the bottom. Then come back to it next
  week.
- **Wins**: don't be shy. Write *every* moment that felt magical. Re-read
  it on hard days.
